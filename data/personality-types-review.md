## The voice standard

Recorded in CLAUDE.md as the standing rule for all user-facing personality text.

- 2–3 sentences.
- Exactly **one** concrete, picturable scene.
- Exactly **one** gentle cost or shadow clause.
- British-casual register.
- **Banned:** "thrive", "whether it's", "deep satisfaction", "unleash", "dive into",
  "passion for", consecutive sentences starting with "You", and any claim the type's
  own axes do not support.

That last clause is the one that bites hardest. The Detour-Taker sits at Energy 18, so
their copy cannot promise anything strenuous however well it would read; the League
Secretary sits at Outdoors 11, so nothing outdoors.

## The mechanism these are attached to

- **All-Rounder** when the strongest and weakest axes are within **F = 8** raw-sum points.
- otherwise a **hybrid** when the second axis is within **H = 3** of the first *and* that pair is named.
- otherwise the **pure** dominant axis.

Shares are over all 81,920 answer paths. Archetype = the single most
type-defining vector the quiz can actually produce for that type, as raw sums; it is what
the hero draws under that name.
