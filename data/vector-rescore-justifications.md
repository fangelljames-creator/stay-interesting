# Stage 4 — every score that moved by ≥ 2, and why

Re-scored against **The 7-axis rubric** in CLAUDE.md. **No wording changed anywhere**: the meaning of
every option is exactly what it was, only the honesty of its numbers changed. 33 options × 7 axes =
231 scores reviewed; **38 moved by ≥ 2**, and 7 options came through with no move of 2 or more.

Axis order throughout: `[Social, Energy, Creative, Analytical, Outdoors, Novelty, Stimulation]`.

The governing principle, and the source of most of these: **score the described behaviour, not its
side effects.**

---

## Q1 — a completely free Saturday morning

**Rally the group** `[9,9,2,2,8,4,8]` → `[9,9,1,1,5,3,6]`
- `Outdoors 8→5` — nothing in "a crowd, loud and physical" says outdoors; a five-a-side pitch and a
  sports hall both fit, which is precisely the rubric's 5.
- `Stimulation 8→6` — the liveliness is already counted on Social 9 and Energy 9. No stakes described.

**Coffee and a puzzle** `[1,2,2,9,1,3,4]` → `[1,1,1,10,1,3,2]`
- `Stimulation 4→2` — a Saturday sudoku over coffee is quiet concentration.

**Out the door, headphones in** `[1,7,2,2,8,2,2]` → `[1,5,1,1,8,2,1]`
- `Energy 7→5` — the description says a long walk or an **easy** ride. Sustained but explicitly
  unstrenuous: the rubric's "light movement involved".

## The pattern

**Stimulation lost 17 of the 38 points-of-movement, and gained one.** It had been acting as a general
"this is exciting" tax on options whose described behaviour is social (Q7 "Drown it out with people"),
creative (Q6 "A performance art"), or entirely passive (Q2 "Sink into a story"). Its option-pool
average falls 5.03 → 3.80 and its floor 2.88 → 1.50, because for the first time options that are
explicitly calm — "the rest of the world goes quiet", "no signal and no agenda", "very little
thought" — are scored at the 1 the rubric gives them.

**Genuine thrill was left alone**, which is the check that this was a re-score and not a haircut:
"Something that scares you a little" keeps 10, "A demanding physical sport" keeps 9, "Find something
to win" keeps 8, "A live, open debate" and "In sync with a team" keep 7 and gain one. And "Physical
exhaustion" was raised, because heaviness is intensity.
